Recording HUD

A content-sized pill, 36px tall, floated 9px below the region. While recording it is ~132px wide — small enough to sit over a desktop for an hour without being in the way, big enough that Stop is never a hard target.

The old bar carried REC, the format name and the dimensions for the entire recording. All three are answers to questions you ask once, before you start — not while you are working. The dot says recording, the timer says how long, and the frame on screen says what. Everything else was decoration at 400px wide.
