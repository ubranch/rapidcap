Panel

400 × ~290. Three stacked rows became a 36px footer: two free-standing chips on the left, status text on the right, nothing boxed together. 138px of panel for 36px.

The earlier version welded all three into one segmented strip, and it was wrong for a reason no amount of spacing fixes — those are three different kinds of thing. No audio is a toggle, Documents is an action, Ready is a readout. A shared box with hairline dividers claims they are peers, so the eye reads one crowded control and the hand cannot tell what is pressable. Chips look pressable because they are; the status has no box because you cannot press it.
