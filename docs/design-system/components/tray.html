Tray

RapidCap minimises to tray, so for most of its life this 16px square is the product. It is currently a flat blue block — Icon::from_rgba([34, 92, 197, 255].repeat(16 * 16)) in platform.rs:263 — with no state, no shape and no relationship to the app.
