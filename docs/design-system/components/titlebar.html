Titlebar

44px tall. Two clusters with different rules: app actions are 36 × 36 rounded buttons on a 4px rhythm, window controls are 46 × 44 full-bleed squares with no gap at all. Everything not a button is drag surface.

The 4px comes from the bar itself — a 36px button in a 44px bar leaves 4px above and below, so 4px between buttons makes the spacing square. App actions read as a group of tiles instead of a crowded strip; window controls stay flush because that is the platform convention and it keeps close on the corner.
